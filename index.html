~ WHO WE ARE

A community-centred organisation
building sustainable change

Sustainable Global Impact (SGI) is based in Rongai, Nakuru. We work with farmers, schools, women groups, and youth at Kayanet Farm to find local solutions that work.

Our promise: We listen to communities first. We use simple, proven ideas. We show results.

Kayanet Farm - Our Learning Centre

600+ farmers trained in climate-smart farming. This is where we test what works, then share with communities.

~ WHAT WE DO

Our Focus Areas

Five connected areas to build strong livelihoods

Sustainable Agriculture

Climate-smart farming, seeds, soil health, and market for small farmers.

Poverty Eradication

Women savings groups, business training, and jobs for youth.

Education & Youth

School support, reading, computers, and youth leaders.

Environment & Climate

Tree planting, water care, and farming that protects nature.

Research & Learning (NEW)

We study what works at Kayanet Farm and share lessons with farmers and partners.
