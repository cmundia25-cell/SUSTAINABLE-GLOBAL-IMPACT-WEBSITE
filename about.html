About SGI

Sustainable Global Impact (SGI) is based in Rongai, Nakuru. Kayanet Farm is our agroecology model farm promoting climate resilience and sustainable livelihoods. Reached over 600 smallholder farmers and students from Kayanet Primary School.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

Healthy, food-secure communities living in harmony with nature and building sustainable futures.

Our Mission

To strengthen food security, protect the environment, and build resilient communities through locally driven solutions at Kayanet Farm in Rongai.

Core Values

Community First • Sustainability • Accountability • Learning & Sharing • Inclusion (Women, Youth)
